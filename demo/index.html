Research demo, not a safety system. A box means the model thinks it sees that class; it can be wrong, especially in the dark, in fog, or when a person is partly hidden. It makes no claim about whether a person is alive, conscious or unhurt. Preprocessing options: CLAHE and adaptive gamma (low light, they did not help on real photographs in the experiments), dehaze (helped on fog, but is slow). Frames stay on this computer.
